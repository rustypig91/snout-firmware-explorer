Rusty's Snout - Firmware Explorer

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers the Microsoft Store and direct-download editions of Rusty's Snout - Firmware Explorer and its command-line tool, developed by Christoffer Zakrisson (rustypig91).

Snout analyzes firmware on your computer. It does not upload firmware, reports, settings or snapshots to the developer. No account is required, and the app includes no advertising, usage analytics or automatic crash-report uploads.

Data the app uses

  • Firmware and supporting files: Snout scans build folders you open and reads selected ELF images, linker maps and compiler stack reports. These can contain firmware bytes, symbols, source paths, compiler information and other proprietary or personal information. The app uses them to calculate and display analysis locally.
  • Preferences: Snout saves recent build-folder paths, selected firmware and supporting files, workspace choices and update preferences in a local workspace file.
  • Baselines: When you save a baseline, Snout stores a local analysis snapshot containing symbols, paths, sizes, addresses, warnings and available stack information. Baselines omit the raw memory image but may still contain sensitive metadata.
  • Files and clipboard: File dialogs let you choose local files and folders. Copy actions use your system clipboard. Command-line reports are written to standard output, which you may redirect or share.
  • Diagnostics: Startup and error output may include graphics adapter and driver details, file paths and error messages. Snout does not automatically send this output to the developer.

Storage and deletion

Workspace preferences and baselines are stored under your user configuration directory. The Store edition uses separate storage from the direct-download edition. Use Menu → Open configuration folder to find the directory visible to your edition. Windows may redirect Store storage into the package's per-user storage.

Saved baselines remain until you delete them or the app cleans up snapshots for build folders no longer present in the saved workspace. To remove all saved app data, close Snout and delete its configuration directory. Remove any exported reports and backups separately. Do not rely on uninstallation to preserve Store data; back up important baselines first.

Snout does not add encryption to local files. Access depends on your operating system and filesystem permissions. Synced folders and backups may retain additional copies. The developer cannot access or delete files held only on your computer.

Network access and external services

Microsoft Store edition: Snout does not contact its GitHub updater. Windows and Microsoft Store manage distribution and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Direct-download desktop editions: Startup update checks contact GitHub by default. You can disable them in the app menu or use --no-update-check for one launch. Manual checks also contact GitHub; choosing Update downloads release files from GitHub's infrastructure. Requests expose your IP address and request information, including the app version in its user-agent, to those services. They do not include firmware, local paths, reports or baselines. The command-line tool has no updater. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Opening project, privacy, support, donation or Store links uses your browser or a system app. Information provided to those services is governed by their own policies.

Information you choose to share

If you contact the developer or submit an issue, the developer receives the message, contact details and attachments you provide and uses them to respond and investigate the report. Support correspondence may be retained while needed to resolve and follow up on the request. You can request deletion of correspondence held by the developer using the address below, subject to applicable legal obligations.

GitHub issues are public. Review firmware, reports and diagnostics before sharing them, and remove sensitive information. Snout does not sell personal information or share local app data with advertisers.

This page and contact

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages without analytics, tracking scripts, external fonts or embedded third-party content. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information such as your IP address under its privacy policy.
